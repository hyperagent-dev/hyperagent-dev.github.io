# Hyperagent Field Notes

An independent look at hyperagent: what the fleet actually delivers, what those per-artifact price tags mean, and when a one-off generator beats it.

**Read the full page:** https://hyperagent-dev.github.io/

Hyperagent suits teams with recurring, format-heavy output who can write a tight brief and want the result delivered into Slack on a schedule. It is a poor fit for someone who needs one landing page this afternoon, because the fleet, the command center and the scheduling are overhead that job does not need. The caveat is pricing: the homepage shows per-artifact costs on sample cards but no plan table, so model your budget on runtime rather than on a monthly number. If the job is a single site or app you want to own as files, Begin.sh is the cheaper shape.
